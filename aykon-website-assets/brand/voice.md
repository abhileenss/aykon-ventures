# Voice & Verbal Identity

The cheapest way to be expensive is voice. This file is the focused reference — pull from here whenever writing copy for the site or generating content.

---

## The five principles

### 01 · Certain, never salesy
Aykon speaks like a principal who's already decided, not a broker hoping you will. Declarative. No urgency, no exclamation, no "act now." **The cost:** we forfeit the conversion tricks that work short-term. **Why:** certainty is the premium signal.

### 02 · Specific, never spreadsheet-y
Use the address, the material, the square metre, the year, the stake — concrete proof. **Never promise a return** or quote a yield as a hook. **The cost:** we lose the punchy "12% ROI!" line every competitor leans on. **Why:** naming a guaranteed number is what amateurs do, and it's often non-compliant.

### 03 · Plain about alignment
Say the thing nobody says: *our name is on it, here's the small stake we put in, here's the RERA partner.* Radical transparency where the category is deliberately vague. **The cost:** disclosure is uncomfortable. **Why:** transparency is the moat.

### 04 · Bilingual, never translated
Arabic and English written in parallel by writers fluent in both. A house claiming a stake in an Arab city cannot speak it with an accent. **The cost:** slower, costlier copy. **Why:** translation is a code-smell.

### 05 · Editorial, never listings-coded
Aykon reads like a private bank's letter to clients or an architecture monograph — not a property portal. No "JUST LISTED," no emoji, no skyline hashtags. **The cost:** we look slow next to the reels. **Why:** restraint is what compounds.

## Sample copy · off-brand vs on-brand

Use these as the reference for every piece of copy that goes on the site.

### Instagram / thesis post
- **Off:** 🔥 HOT DEAL in Business Bay! DM for price 📲 Limited units!!
- **On:** We're backing a floor in Business Bay this month. Here's the thesis — and the RERA partner.

### Deal announcement
- **Off:** Aykon is proud to announce an exciting new acquisition!
- **On:** Our name is on a floor in DIFC. Our stake is in. The RERA partner is named below.

### Partner / co-invest one-pager
- **Off:** Guaranteed 15% ROI! Don't miss this opportunity.
- **On:** We hold a small stake in this asset with our own money. You can invest beside us, same terms, same risk, executed through [RERA Partner].

### Aykon Jewels
- **Off:** Stunning 22k gold set — best price in Dubai!
- **On:** Designed in-house. Set by hand. Worth more for the eye than the gram.

### Aykon Spaces
- **Off:** We create stunning luxury dream spaces for elite clients!
- **On:** We design what we'd put our own name on. Some of it, we will.

### When something slips
- **Off:** We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience caused!!
- **On:** The deal didn't clear diligence. We walked. Your name was never on it.

## Tagline ladder (four kept live for A/B)

No single tagline is locked at the platform layer. Each owns a different surface (short → relational → declarative → quiet). Real-world performance picks the winner over the first 90 days of public-facing content.

| Tagline | Best surface | Voice register |
|---|---|---|
| **Skin in.** | OOH, ad ends, social sign-offs | Sharpest, two-word brand close |
| **Same side of the table.** | Partner conversations, editorial | Relational, explicitly anti-broker |
| **We back what we sell.** | Website hero, formal documents | Most declarative |
| **Name on it.** | Quiet sign-offs under any visual | Confidence; reputation as alignment |

**Default for the website hero:** *We back what we sell.* Treat as a config value so it can rotate during A/B.

## Banned words (any one = off-brand)

### Words
- `luxury`
- `premium`
- `elite`
- `exclusive`
- `trusted`
- `leading`
- `partner` (as filler/buzzword)
- `guaranteed`
- `ROI` (as a hook)
- `don't miss`
- `dream home`
- `dream investment`
- `fund` (Aykon is not one)
- `syndicate` (Aykon is not licensed for syndication)
- `AUM`
- `portfolio company`

### Patterns
- Exclamation marks in body copy
- Emoji in brand copy (permitted in private internal Slack only)
- Title Case Headlines (sentence case always)
- Skyline hashtags
- "DM for price"
- Three-adjective stacks ("Quick, easy, affordable.")
- Rhetorical questions ("Tired of renting?")
- Urgency manufacturing ("only X left," countdowns)

## Sub-brand voice modulation

- **Aykon Ventures (parent)** — the default voice above. Plain about alignment.
- **Aykon Jewels** — quieter, more sensory. Talks about the hand, the design, the stone. Never souk-coded, never per-gram.
- **Aykon Spaces** — monograph register — spatial, slow, considered. Lets the work speak.

## Three things NOT to invent

When the website's content gets written, these three must be sourced from real humans/decisions, never machine-generated:

1. **Real Arabic copy.** Stub everything as `// TODO: native Arabic copy lead`. A bilingual native writer produces final Arabic — never translate by tool.
2. **Promised returns.** Banned by voice. Show stakes, partners, status — never a guaranteed yield.
3. **RERA partner names.** Leave as `[Partner Legal Name]` placeholder until Prachi confirms the actual contractual partners.
